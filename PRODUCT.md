# Madalyn Robinson Foundation

## Register

**brand** — public foundation marketing and event registration. Admin is a thin product surface serving the brand site.

## Users & purpose

- **Visitors / community:** Learn Maddy’s story, find events, register to attend, donate when ready.
- **Staff (admin):** Create and manage custom events and view registration rosters.

Primary job: make it easy to gather people around Madalyn’s light — events first, story always nearby.

## Brand personality

Warm · hopeful · steadfast

Emotions: calm courage, joy without denial, invitation rather than urgency.

## Anti-references

- Generic nonprofit “purple gradient + Inter” templates
- Cream/sand paper editorial AI look
- Dense dashboard chrome on the public site
- Card grids and pill-stat strips in the hero

## Strategic principles

1. Maddy’s presence leads — photo and name before features.
2. One job per section; hero stays lean (brand, headline, line, CTAs, image).
3. Registration is phone-simple; admin is boring and reliable.
4. Domain `maddysfoundation.org` is the product name on the web when DNS lands.
